Data Requests

Effective date: October 13, 2026. Last updated: October 13, 2026.

Use this page to ask Verba to delete, correct, or provide a copy of personal information we hold about you, or to unsubscribe you from email. In practice, the only personal information Verba holds is the email address of newsletter subscribers, any messages you have sent us, and any information you entered into the earlier Glide-hosted Verba app. The MVP on the home page sends Verba nothing. If you turn on progress tracking, your session history stays in your own browser, and you can delete it yourself from the "Your progress" panel on the home page.

How it works

  1. Fill in the form below. Selecting "Prepare email" opens your email app with a message addressed to baig.h@northeastern.edu. Nothing is sent until you send that email yourself, and this page does not transmit or store what you type.
  2. We may reply to confirm that you control the email address in the request. We will not ask for more information than we need to verify you.
  3. We will complete the request, or explain why we cannot, within 45 days. You can appeal a decision as described in the Privacy Policy.

If the form does not open your email app, write to baig.h@northeastern.edu with the subject line "Privacy Request" and include the same details.

This is the address we will search for and contact.

For corrections, tell us what is wrong. Please do not include passwords or sensitive information.
