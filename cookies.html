Cookie Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026. Last updated: October 13, 2026.

Summary. Verba sets no cookies of its own and uses no advertising or analytics trackers. It stores two small values in your browser's local storage. The beehiiv newsletter form, which may set cookies, loads only if you allow it.

1. What these technologies are

Cookies are small text files that websites place on your device. Local storage is a similar browser feature that lets a website keep small pieces of information on your device. This policy uses "cookies" to refer to both.

2. Storage used by Verba

NameTypePurposeDurationCategory
verba_consentLocal storageRemembers whether you accepted or rejected optional third-party contentUntil you clear site dataStrictly necessary
verba_disclaimer_seenLocal storageRemembers that you have read the browser demo notice, so it is not shown each timeUntil you clear site dataFunctional, set only when you start the demo
verba_history_optLocal storageRemembers whether you turned progress tracking on or offUntil you clear site dataFunctional, set only when you choose
verba_historyLocal storageYour saved practice sessions (numbers only: date, length, pace, filler and hedge counts, pauses, score), shown in the "Your progress" panelUntil you delete it from the panel or clear site dataFunctional, only if you turn on progress tracking
verba-* cacheCache storage (service worker)A copy of the Site's own pages, styles and scripts so it can open offline and load fasterReplaced when the Site updates; removed when you clear site dataFunctional

These values stay on your device. Verba never reads them from a server, and they do not identify you.

4. Other services

  • Fonts are hosted on the Verba Site itself. No request is made to Google Fonts or any other font service.
  • Speech recognition in the browser demo is performed by your browser and its provider, under the provider's own policies. Verba sets no cookies for it.
  • External links to the Glide-hosted MVP take you to a site that sets its own cookies under its own policies. Nothing from that site loads on Verba pages.
  • Hosting. GitHub Pages serves the Site and may log technical information for security. It does not set cookies on Verba pages.

5. Managing your choice

You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie Settings" link at the bottom of every page. You can also delete local storage and cookies through your browser settings. If you withdraw consent, the beehiiv form is removed the next time the page loads, and the form will not appear until you allow it again.
