Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026. Last updated: October 13, 2026.

Summary. The on-page demo does not record or store your voice, and Verba's own code sends no audio or transcripts to any Verba server. Your browser's built-in speech recognition service may process your audio on the browser maker's servers. We collect your email address only if you join our newsletter, and you can unsubscribe at any time. We do not sell personal information, show advertising, or use analytics cookies.

1. Who we are

Verba ("Verba," "we," "us") is an independent student project operated by Haroon Baig in Connecticut, United States. This policy covers the Verba website at https://haroonbaig314-lgtm.github.io/Verba/ (the "Site"), the Verba MVP (the browser-based practice tool on the Site), the Verba newsletter, and an earlier Verba app that was hosted on Glide. Questions about this policy can be sent to baig.h@northeastern.edu.

2. Information we collect

2.1 The MVP (browser-based practice tool)

When you press the microphone button, your browser asks for permission to use your microphone. If you grant it:

  • Speech recognition. The demo uses the Web Speech API built into your browser. Your browser, not Verba, converts your speech to text. Depending on the browser, this processing may take place on the browser provider's servers (for example, Google for Chrome, Microsoft for Edge, and Apple for Safari) and is governed by that provider's privacy policy.
  • Transcript and statistics. The transcript, filler word counts, words per minute, and session summary exist only in your browser's memory while the page is open. Verba does not record your audio, does not save the transcript, and does not transmit either to Verba or to any other party. Reloading or closing the page erases them.
  • Volume meter and "um" detection. The microphone level meter and the detection of hesitation sounds such as "um" and "uh" are calculated locally in your browser from the live audio signal. That audio is analyzed as it arrives and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

2.2 Newsletter

If you subscribe to the newsletter, we collect the email address you enter. The signup form and mailing list are provided by beehiiv, Inc., which processes your email address on our behalf and may collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and email open and click activity, as described in beehiiv's privacy policy. The form loads only after you allow third-party content (see our Cookie Policy).

2.3 Earlier Glide-hosted app

An earlier version of Verba was a separate application hosted on Glide (glideapps.com). The Site no longer links to it. If you used it, the information you entered and the recordings you submitted were stored by Glide and sent to OpenAI for transcription (Whisper) and analysis (GPT) in order to produce your feedback. You can ask us to delete that information through the data request page. Glide's and OpenAI's handling of that data is governed by their respective privacy policies and data processing terms. Do not submit recordings that contain sensitive personal information about yourself or others.

2.4 Information stored on your device

The Site stores a few values in your browser's local storage: verba_disclaimer_seen, which records that you have read the demo notice; verba_consent, which records your cookie choice; and verba_theme, which remembers whether you chose dark mode. If you turn on progress tracking, it also stores verba_history_opt (your choice) and verba_history, a list of your past sessions containing only numbers: date, length, pace, filler and hedge counts, pauses and score. Your words and audio are never stored. To make the Site work offline, your browser may also keep a copy of the Site's own files in its cache storage. None of this identifies you or is sent to us, and you can delete your history from the "Your progress" panel or by clearing site data. Details are in the Cookie Policy.

2.4a Results images

The "Share your result" buttons draw an image of your score, pace, filler rate and pause count in your browser. The image is saved to your device or passed to your device's share menu; Verba never receives it. It contains no words from your session.

2.5 Hosting logs

The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information, including visitor IP addresses, for security and operational purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive or use these logs.

2.6 When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message.

2.7 What we do not collect

We do not use analytics tools, advertising networks, tracking pixels, or social media tracking code. We do not ask for your name, location, payment details, or any sensitive personal information on the Site. Fonts are served from our own Site, so loading a page does not send your information to a font provider.

3. How we use information

  • To send the newsletter and product updates you asked to receive.
  • To respond to your messages and to privacy requests.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the Site and the MVP.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for targeted advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you.

4. Legal bases for processing (EEA and UK visitors)

We rely on your consent for the newsletter and for loading third-party content, and you may withdraw consent at any time. We rely on our legitimate interests to respond to messages and to keep the Site secure, and on legal obligation where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

6. How long we keep information

  • Newsletter email address: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. After you unsubscribe, beehiiv may keep a suppression record so that you are not emailed again.
  • Emails to us: up to 24 months after the conversation ends, unless a longer period is required by law.
  • Privacy request records: up to 24 months, to show that we handled the request.
  • Demo data: not retained. It is erased when you leave or reload the page.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including under the Connecticut Data Privacy Act, the California Consumer Privacy Act, and the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation, you may have the right to:

  • confirm whether we process your personal information and obtain a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate personal information;
  • delete your personal information;
  • receive your personal information in a portable format;
  • opt out of the sale of personal information, targeted advertising, and profiling (we do none of these);
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that took place before withdrawal; and
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

We extend these rights to every visitor regardless of location. We will not treat you differently for exercising them.

How to make a request

Use the data request page or email baig.h@northeastern.edu with the subject line "Privacy Request." Tell us what you would like us to do and the email address associated with your information. We may ask you to confirm that you control that email address before acting on the request. An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf with your signed permission.

We will respond within 45 days. If we need more time, we will tell you why, and the total period will not exceed 90 days. When we delete newsletter data, we remove it from beehiiv and ask any other provider holding it to do the same.

Appeals

If we decline your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision with the subject line "Privacy Appeal." We will respond to an appeal within 60 days. If you are not satisfied with the outcome, Connecticut residents may contact the Connecticut Attorney General at portal.ct.gov/ag.

8. Email communications

We send email only to people who subscribe. Every newsletter email contains an unsubscribe link, and you can also unsubscribe by emailing baig.h@northeastern.edu. We process unsubscribe requests promptly and within 10 business days at most.

9. Children's privacy

The Site and the newsletter are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. You must be at least 13 to subscribe to the newsletter, and users under 18 should have a parent or guardian's permission. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact baig.h@northeastern.edu and we will delete it promptly. The MVP collects no personal information from any user.

10. Security

The Site is served over HTTPS. We limit the personal information we hold and rely on established providers to store it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International transfers

Verba and its providers are located in the United States. If you access Verba from outside the United States, your information will be processed in the United States, where data protection law may differ from the law in your country.

12. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above. If a change materially affects how we use information we already hold, we will notify newsletter subscribers by email before the change takes effect.
