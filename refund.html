Refund Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026. Last updated: October 13, 2026.

Verba is free right now. We do not sell any plan, collect payment details, or charge any fee, so there is nothing to refund today. This page explains the terms that will apply if we introduce paid plans.

1. Current status

The Verba MVP and the newsletter are free. Prices on the Site, such as the planned $6 per month, describe intended future pricing and are not offers to sell. No one is authorized to collect payment for Verba on our behalf. If you are ever asked to pay for Verba before paid plans are announced on this Site, do not pay and contact baig.h@northeastern.edu.

2. Terms for future paid plans

If we launch paid plans, the following terms will apply unless a different policy is shown to you at checkout:

  • Clear pricing. The full price in U.S. dollars, the billing period, any applicable taxes, and the renewal terms will be shown before you pay. We will not add fees that were not disclosed at checkout.
  • Free tier. We intend to offer a free tier, and the features included in it will be described before purchase.
  • Cancel anytime. You can cancel a subscription at any time through the same method you used to subscribe, or by emailing baig.h@northeastern.edu. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and there is no cancellation fee.
  • 14-day refund on first purchase. If you are not satisfied with your first paid subscription, you may request a full refund within 14 days of the initial charge.
  • Billing errors. Duplicate charges, charges after a confirmed cancellation, and other billing errors will be refunded in full.
  • Renewals. Renewal charges are generally not refundable once a new billing period begins, except where required by law or covered above. We will send a reminder before any annual renewal.
  • Refund method. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days.

3. App store purchases

If a future version of Verba is purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play, refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies. We will help where we can.

4. Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under the law where you live, including any right of withdrawal available to consumers in the European Union or the United Kingdom.

5. How to request a refund

Email baig.h@northeastern.edu with the subject line "Refund Request," the email address on your account, and the date of the charge. We will reply within 5 business days.
